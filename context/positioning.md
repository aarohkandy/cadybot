**The line.** Other companies make something flashy. We make something that
works and gives you what you actually asked for.

Use that framing. It is the whole differentiator and it should survive contact
with any messaging recommendation.

## The competitive gap we sit in

- **Engineering text-to-CAD** — Zoo (formerly KittyCAD), AdamCAD (YC W25, $4.1M
  seed), Spectral Labs' SGS-1. Aimed at engineers who want parametric B-Rep CAD.
  Powerful, and not built for someone who just wants a bracket that fits.
- **Text-to-3D mesh generators** — Meshy, Tripo, Sloyd. Aimed at artists who
  want good-looking meshes for games and renders. Output frequently needs mesh
  repair before it will print, and is not dimensionally trustworthy.
- **The gap.** A printable functional part needs watertight geometry, adequate
  wall thickness, and real dimensions. Engineering CAD tools are too hard for a
  hobbyist; mesh generators don't produce printable geometry. Nobody serves
  "I own a printer and want a thing that fits *my* shelf."

That gap is the position. Recommendations should reinforce it, not blur it.
