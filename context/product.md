AI text-to-CAD. You describe what you want; it produces a printable 3D model.

**Who it's for.** People who own a 3D printer and either don't know what to print
with it, or can't print exactly the thing they want. Not primarily engineers,
though engineers and people modifying things around the house are a plausible
second audience.

**The core promise.** Your idea becomes a real object. You should not need to
learn CAD to get a part that actually fits.

**Status.** Not finished. Do not recommend anything that assumes a polished,
self-serve product.
